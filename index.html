⚡ What We Offer

Our Services

Professional solar and electrical services tailored to your needs.

🪟

Solar Panel Installation

Professional design and mounting of solar panels for homes, businesses, and institutions. We handle everything from roof assessment to final connection, ensuring maximum sunlight exposure and optimal energy generation for your property.

🔋

Solar Battery Storage

Backup systems to store excess solar energy for use during power outages, nighttime, or cloudy days. We install high-capacity lithium-ion and lead-acid batteries that automatically switch on when grid power fails.

⚡

Inverter Installation

Reliable conversion of solar DC power into usable AC electricity for your appliances. We supply and install pure sine wave, hybrid, and off-grid inverters sized perfectly for your energy consumption needs.

💧

Solar Water Heating

Eco‑friendly hot water solutions that reduce electricity bills by up to 70%. We install both compact and split system solar water heaters for residential and commercial properties.

📐

System Design & Consultation

Customized solar setups based on your energy needs, load analysis, roof space, and budget. Our engineers provide free site visits and detailed proposals before any installation begins.

🛠️

Maintenance & Repairs

Regular servicing, panel cleaning, system inspections, and troubleshooting to keep your solar system running at peak efficiency. We offer warranty support and emergency repair services.
